🌀 Tuple:
  🔹 Tuples are ordered collections of items, similar to lists, but they are immutable, meaning their elements cannot be changed after creation.
  🔹 Defined using parentheses `( )`, with elements separated by commas.
  🔹 Useful for representing fixed collections of items like coordinates, dates, or configurations.
  🔹 Support operations like indexing, slicing, and iteration, but do not support methods that modify the tuple.

🛑 Set:
  🔸 Sets are unordered collections of unique items.
  🔸 Defined using curly braces `{ }`, or the `set()` constructor.
  🔸 Automatically remove duplicate values, making them ideal for tasks like removing duplicates from a list.
  🔸 Support set operations like union, intersection, and difference, leveraging mathematical set theory.
  🔸 Useful for membership testing and eliminating duplicate entries from data.
